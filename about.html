Meet the studio

Craft-led ideas, built for real-time worlds.

Oblix Studio brings animation, game development, asset creation and digital media into one focused production practice. The aim is not to imitate large agencies—it is to make distinctive work with clarity, detail and genuine enthusiasm for the medium.

01

Distinct visual direction

Every project should feel authored. We develop a visual language around the story, audience and experience rather than relying on a default house style.

02

Playable thinking

Ideas are considered through interaction, movement, camera, environment and player experience—not only how they look in a still frame.

03

Production awareness

Assets and systems are designed with practical pipelines, optimisation and implementation in mind from the start.

04

Direct collaboration

Clients and collaborators work directly with the person making the work, keeping decisions clear and momentum high.
